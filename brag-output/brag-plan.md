# brag-plan — Grocery Admin

**What it is:** a cross-platform (Expo / React Native) supermarket admin app for billing staff to manage Stock, Purchase and Sale.
**Angle:** the real app doing its job — splash to sign-in, a 4-digit PIN, the dashboard, a swipe to any action, a bill from cart to checkout.
**Hook:** the real splash (shelves gliding in) — poster frame is the settled dashboard.
**Punchline / outro:** `grocery` wordmark + "Stock · Purchase · Sale" (the app's three modules).
**Tone:** default — punchy, playful, clean. 1920×1080, 30fps, 21.7s.
**Identity:** charcoal #2A2A30, cyan #1EB7EB, green #1ED58A, cream #FFF6E9; Bricolage Grotesque (the app's own display font).

## Notes
- The dashboard and catalog use small illustrative product/branch/supplier data served through request interception (the live API was not called). The Bill summary total is whatever the two mocked items add up to.
- Sound: Am–F–C–G pad, pluck arpeggio, soft kick/hat at 100 bpm; effects (PIN blips, loader whoosh, picker ticks, checkout chime) are in the same key.
